Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Klipcy (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Klipcy Shopify app (the “Shopify App”) and the Klipcy Mobile App (together, the “App”). Klipcy helps Shopify merchants generate, customize, and manage invoices for their store’s orders. The Shopify App is installed and used inside the Shopify Admin, and the Mobile App is a companion Android/iOS app that lets a store’s registered owner view that store’s orders, receipts, and invoices on their phone. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to operate the App. Depending on how you use it, this may include the following:

  1. Store information: your store domain, store name, email address, currency, timezone, and basic store configuration, obtained through the Shopify APIs.
  2. Order information: order numbers, line items, totals, taxes, discounts, shipping details, and fulfillment status.
  3. Customer information (Protected Customer Data): your customers’ names, email addresses, phone numbers, and billing and shipping addresses, as contained in your orders.
  4. App data: the invoice templates, branding settings, and invoices you create within the App.
  5. Owner login information (Mobile App): the store domain and owner email address you enter at login, used solely to verify that you are the store’s registered owner, and a one-time login code sent to that email. The Mobile App does not use passwords. The code is stored temporarily on our servers in a hashed (non-reversible) form and automatically expires after 5 minutes.
  6. Push notification token (Mobile App): if you allow notifications, a device push token provided by Expo, our push notification service. It identifies your device for notification delivery only and is removed from our systems when you log out of the Mobile App.

How We Use Information We Collect

We use the information we collect only to provide and secure the App, specifically to:

  • generate, format, and manage invoices for your orders;
  • store and apply your invoice templates and branding settings;
  • authenticate your store and keep your session secure;
  • deliver invoices (for example, by email) when you request it;
  • verify that the person logging into the Mobile App is the store’s registered owner and send them a one-time login code; and
  • send the store owner a push notification when an order is created or its status changes, if notifications are enabled in the Mobile App.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data or your customers’ data for advertising or marketing purposes.

We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third-party websites, and this Privacy Policy does not apply to them. Cookies & Logs

The Shopify App uses only the cookies and session tokens required to authenticate your store and keep you securely signed in while you use the App. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. Our hosting infrastructure may keep standard technical logs (such as request time and error information) for the purpose of operating, securing, and troubleshooting the App.

Disclosure of User Information to Third Parties

We share information only with the service providers necessary to operate the App:

  • Shopify — the platform the App runs on and the source of store and order data.
  • Supabase — managed database hosting.
  • Vercel — application hosting.
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications to the Klipcy Mobile App.

These providers process information solely to provide their services and are bound by their own privacy and security commitments. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Klipcy, our users, or others.

Exercising Your Rights regarding Personal Data

Merchants and their customers may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data. Klipcy supports Shopify’s mandatory compliance webhooks for customer privacy:

  • Customer data request — we respond to requests for the data we hold about a customer.
  • Customer redaction — we delete a customer’s personal data on request.
  • Shop redaction — we delete a store’s data after the App is uninstalled.

Requests submitted through Shopify are handled automatically. You may also make a request by contacting us at the email address below.

Security

Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase, and the App is hosted on Vercel. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on infrastructure that employs encryption at rest. Access to data is restricted to what is needed to operate the App and is protected by access keys. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Additional Information

Data retention. We retain data only for as long as the App is installed and the data is necessary to provide its functionality. When you uninstall the App, your store’s data is deleted in accordance with Shopify’s mandatory data-deletion (shop/redact) webhook, typically within 48 hours of uninstall. Mobile App login codes expire after 5 minutes, and push tokens are removed when you log out.
